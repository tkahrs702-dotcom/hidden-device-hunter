## Signals between boards
| Link | Signal | Level | Direction | Wires |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R4 <-> XIAO (Phase 2) | UART 115200 8N1 | 5 V R4 / 3.3 V XIAO via shifter | both (R4 -> XIAO unused so far) | S1, S2 (R4 D1 -> HV1/LV1 -> XIAO D7); S3, S4 (XIAO D6 -> LV2/HV2 -> R4 D0); grounds P4, P5 |
| XIAO <-> P4 (Phase 3) | UART 115200 8N1 | 3.3 V | both | T3 (XIAO D6 -> P4 pin 11 GPIO5); T4 (P4 pin 14 GPIO4 -> XIAO D7); ground T2 |
| P4 -> R4 (optional) | UART | 3.3 V to 5 V via shifter ch 3 | one way | O1, O4 (P4 pin 8 GPIO2 -> LV3/HV3 -> R4 D0) |
| BNO085 <-> P4 | I2C 0x4A | 3.3 V | both | I2 SDA pin 4, I3 SCL pin 6 |
| R4 -> buzzer | tone, about 2.5 kHz | 5 V | out | W1 D8 via R1 100 ohm |

XIAO -> R4 line protocol (`phase2-xiao-sniffer` -> `phase2-r4-display`), one ASCII line each, `\n` terminated:
| Line | Meaning | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| `L,<MAC>,<cat>,<maker>` | locked onto MAC; cat 0 unknown, 1 camera maker, 2 IoT chip | on lock |
| `U` | unlocked | on unlock and at boot without a saved lock |
| `T,<now>,<avg>,<ch>` | target RSSI now and averaged (dBm), channel | up to 10 per second while heard |
| `S,<total>,<cams>,<iot>,<hidden>,<locked>` | devices heard in the last 60 s | once a second |

## Power
| Board | Powered from | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| R4 | USB-C (computer or power bank) | 5 V pin ~4.7 V on USB; max 8 mA per I/O pin, 60 mA total |
| XIAO (Phase 2) | R4 5V pin via P1 + P3 | never also plug its USB-C; pull P3 first to use its USB |
| XIAO (Phase 3) | P4 header pin 1 (5V) via T1 | never also plug its USB-C; Seeed's "5V dead on battery" refers to the XIAO's own battery pads, not this feed; P4 header 5V comes from its boost on LiPo |
| Shifter | HV from R4 5V, LV from XIAO 3V3 (Phase 2) or P4 pin 18 (Phase 3) | both sides must be powered for data to pass |
| BNO085 | P4 pin 2 (3V3) | |
| P4 | USB-C or MX1.25 LiPo | short-press POWER |

## Board pin cheats
- **R4 (USB-C on the left):** top header SCL SDA AREF GND 13 12 11 10 9 8 | 7 6 5 4 3 2 TX>1 RX<0. Bottom header (blank) IOREF RESET 3.3V 5V GND GND VIN | A0-A5.
  D0 = RX, D1 = TX (`Serial1`). Debug on USB `Serial`. 12x8 LED matrix on board.
- **XIAO ESP32-S3 (USB-C at the top, component side up):** left column D0-D6, right column 5V, GND, 3V3, D10, D9, D8, D7.
  D0=GPIO1, D1=2, D2=3, D3=4, D4=5 (SDA), D5=6 (SCL), D6=43 (TX), D7=44 (RX), D8=7, D9=8, D10=9. BOOT button = GPIO0.
- **Shifter:** see [`level-shifter.svg`](diagrams/level-shifter.svg) and 02 section 2.2.
- **P4 header:** see the full table in [`03-phase3-p4-rf-camera.md`](03-phase3-p4-rf-camera.md).

## Verification status
All items from the original guide are checked against sources (see the log in `00-overview-and-safety.md`).
Two bench confirmations remain, both one-minute multimeter checks done when you have the hardware:
1. Header 5V (pin 1 or 3) reads about 5 V when the P4 runs on LiPo alone (the schematic says yes).
2. LiPo plug: red wire lands on J2 pin 1 (BAT) and black on pin 2 (GND) before you connect it.
Diagrams are drawn from the same sources; physical part positions (R4 matrix, XIAO u.FL socket, P4 connectors) are approximate, pin
order is exact. Generic shifter clones vary in pin order: wire by the printed label.
