# 2026-10-26 session 01YKZUS1: C5 review, phone dashboard

**What happened**
- Reviewed the C5 sniffer and R4 display. Fixed: a quiet locked target sent the C5 into a full sweep, so the Tuya plug (10:5A:17:6C:02:4D) was rarely caught. The C5 now holds its channel 30 s.
- C5 flashed, locked the plug on boot with no typing; R4 showed bars.
- Added: peak dot (closest approach), a phone page from the R4 (Wi-Fi HDH-Finder, http://192.168.4.1) with dBm, graph, recommendation and up to 60 devices, hidden networks ranked with suspects plus a BOOT-presses column.
- PRs #9 to #14 merged to main.

**Lessons**
- Flash the C5 by holding BOOT while plugging in; use the /dev/serial/by-id path, not ttyACM.
- The R4 has little RAM: the 60-device list overflowed it until JSON was built in small pieces. CI catches this.
- Small power banks cut out under the C5; use the 2-port bank.
- Hidden devices that only probe use random MACs and cannot be locked.
